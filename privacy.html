Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Pool Duel is a local two-player billiards game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how privacy is protected.

Information We Collect

Pool Duel does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or other personal information to play the game.

Gameplay Data

The app may store basic game state on your device, such as match progress, settings, or audio preferences. This information is used only to operate the game and is not used to identify you.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

If ads or platform services are enabled in the app, third-party providers may process device or usage information according to their own policies. These services are used to support the app, measure performance, prevent fraud, and keep the service secure.

Children's Privacy

Pool Duel is designed as a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer so it can be addressed.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when required to operate the app, comply with law, prevent abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and the developer.

Data Retention

Local game settings remain on your device until you delete the app or clear the app data. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies.

Your Choices

You can delete locally stored game data by deleting the app from your device. You can also manage advertising and tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, please contact the developer through the App Store listing for Pool Duel.
